      Sync with GitHub

      Keep your progress (note statistics, practice calendar, speed tests, stars and finished chapters) in a private gist on your GitHub account, so it follows you to other computers and browsers. Settings like volume stay on each device.

      1. Create a GitHub token with only the gist permission (the link ticks it for you). Pick an expiry you’re comfortable with.
      2. Paste it here and connect. Do the same on your other devices with the same token.

      The token is stored only in this browser and is sent only to GitHub. Other sites published at the same github.io address could read it, which is why it should have no permission except gists.

      How to play

      • Mouse or touch: click the keys below. Drag across them to glide.
      • Computer keyboard: A S D F G H J K are the white keys, and W E T Y U are the black keys. Z and X move down or up an octave. Hold Space for the sustain pedal.
      • MIDI keyboard: plug it in and press Connect MIDI below. Works in Chrome and Edge.
      • Middle C (C4) has a small dot under it, so you can always find your place.
