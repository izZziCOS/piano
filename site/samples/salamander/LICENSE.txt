Salamander Grand Piano V3 (Yamaha C5)
Author: Alexander Holm

Source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano (original: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3)

Changes made for this website: three of the sixteen velocity layers (v5 = "p",
v10 = "m", v15 = "f") of every third semitone from A0 to C8 were trimmed to
7 seconds with a 1.5-second fade-out, mixed down to mono, and encoded as
96 kbps MP3. Files are named <note>-<layer>.mp3, with "s" for sharp (Ds4 = D#4).
